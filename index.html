THE EDITORIAL · FROM SOMEONE WHO KNOWS HIM

IN OTHER NEWS, HE IS A REALLY GOOD PERSON.

Behind the debates, the late nights, the projects, the food orders, and the brain with approximately a billion tabs open is someone deeply considerate. Someone who goes out of his way to do things people need or want. Someone whose empathy is not a headline or a performance, but a real part of how he moves through the world.

He may not always notice how rational he can be, how carefully he considers people, or how much love he has to give. But it is there, in the big gestures and the tiny details: remembering what someone said, making thoughtful gift cards, bringing snacks, choosing their favourite songs, and worrying because he cares.

For his twenties, the wish is simple: may he get to live the life he has always wanted. May he build things he is proud of, see the places he dreams about, feel happy in the life he is making, and one day look back and be able to say, with his whole chest, I did it.

And in the most important news of all: you are so loved, forever.
